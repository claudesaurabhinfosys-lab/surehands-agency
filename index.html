Our Process

Five careful steps,
one peaceful outcome.

No rushing, no pressure. Just a calm, structured process to bring the right helper home.

  1. 01

    Understand your household needs

    A relaxed conversation — usually on WhatsApp or in person — to understand who lives at home, daily routines, special care needs, and the kind of person who would fit naturally with your family.

  2. 02

    Recommend suitable helpers

    We hand-pick a small shortlist — never an overwhelming list of bio-datas. Each candidate comes with a full, honest profile: experience, language ability, family situation, and our personal notes from interviewing them.

  3. 03

    Interview & selection

    Video or in-person interviews, with our consultant present to translate, clarify, and guide. Take your time — we'd rather you meet a few candidates than rush into a decision you'll second-guess.

  4. 04

    Documentation & onboarding

    We handle MOM applications, medical exams, insurance, and airport pickup. Before your helper arrives, we'll brief you on first-week expectations, house rules, and how to set a calm tone from day one.

  5. 05

    Aftercare & support

    We stay involved long after placement — checking in regularly, mediating misunderstandings, and supporting both family and helper. 6-month guarantee with up to 3 free replacements included.

How much does it cost to hire a helper? +

All prices include insurance and bond. WhatsApp us for a clear, written breakdown before you commit — no hidden charges, ever.

What if the helper isn't the right fit? +

Our placements include a 6-month guarantee with up to 3 free replacements. We always mediate first — small misunderstandings often resolve with a calm conversation. If a replacement is genuinely needed, we re-match thoughtfully, not hastily.

How long does the whole process take? +

A transfer helper can typically arrive in 1–3 weeks. New helpers from overseas usually take 4–8 weeks once paperwork begins. We'll give you realistic timelines from day one.

I'm a first-time employer — is this complicated? +

Not at all. We'll guide you through MOM's Employer Orientation Programme, contracts, house rules, and what to expect in your helper's first week. No question is too basic.

What documents will I need? +

For employers: NRIC, proof of address, and income documentation. For renewals: existing work permit and helper's passport. We handle everything else — work permit application, medical exams, insurance, and onboarding paperwork — on your behalf.

Do you accept instalment payments? +

Yes! We accept Atome, Grab PayLater, Shopee PayLater, and ShopBack PayLater for 1, 3, 6, or 12 month instalments. We also accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB, UnionPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, GrabPay, PayNow, and bank transfer.
